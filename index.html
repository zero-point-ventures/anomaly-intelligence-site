Before you head out

A few things to know.

Does it tell me whether something is paranormal?

Anomaly Intelligence records physical changes and flags what stands out from a room’s measured baseline. An unusual reading doesn’t establish a cause. The recordings and context help you and your AI consider ordinary explanations.

Do I need special equipment?

You can start with your iPhone’s built-in microphone, motion sensors, magnetometer and barometer. Set the phone on a stable surface and let it establish a baseline before investigating.

Does the app automatically send recordings to an AI?

No. Export a finished investigation and choose where to share it yourself. Your recordings stay on your iPhone unless you decide otherwise.
