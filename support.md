# Anomaly Intelligence Support

Anomaly Intelligence records sound and your iPhone's motion and environment sensors during an investigation. It flags anything that stands out from the room's normal readings. It records and reports; it never claims anything is paranormal.

Need help? Email **contact@zpventures.io**. We usually reply within two working days.

## Getting started

1. **Start a new investigation.** Give it a name, write down what the site is said to have, and choose a mode:
   - **Stationary:** the phone sits still for hours and records everything.
   - **EVP:** you ask questions aloud and listen for responses.
2. **Follow the checklist.** Airplane mode, the charger unplugged and Do Not Disturb give the cleanest recording. Anything you skip is noted in the session.
3. **Let the baseline finish.** The phone measures the room's normal levels first, so it can tell what's unusual later.
4. **Keep the phone still.** Moving it registers on several sensors at once. The app marks movement, but a still phone gives the most reliable results.

## Analysing an investigation with your AI

Open a finished session and tap **Export**. The app creates one file containing the measurements, audio clips and a guide written for AI assistants. Send it to the AI assistant you already use through the share sheet, or save it to Files and upload it.

For EVP sessions, the export includes possible words that Apple's speech recogniser heard in each listening window. Each window is also checked played backwards as a control. These are a machine's guesses: weigh them against the controls and listen to the audio yourself.

## Common questions

**Why doesn't my EVP export include any words?** Apple's speech recogniser needs iOS 26 and a supported iPhone. The app offers to download Apple's speech model before an EVP session if it's missing. If you chose "Not now", it asks again next time.

**Does the app send my recordings anywhere?** No. Everything stays on your iPhone unless you share an export yourself. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy).

**Why was an event marked as phone movement?** The motion sensors showed the phone moved. Movement affects several sensors at once, so the app counts it as one ordinary cause.

**Can it measure temperature or cold spots?** No. iPhones don't have a temperature sensor for the air around them. Log what you feel with a mark instead.
